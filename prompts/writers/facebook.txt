Write native Facebook content.

The post should:
- be self-contained;
- provide slightly more context than X;
- remain direct and readable;
- preserve uncertainty and evidence nuance;
- avoid clickbait;
- avoid generic motivational filler.

Choose text, image, or video as the format based on the content itself.
The writer produces copy only; it does not invent an asset that does
not exist.

Use an empty string when CTA is intentionally omitted.
