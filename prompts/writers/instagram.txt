Write an Instagram feed carousel.

The carousel must:
- have 2 to 10 slides;
- open with a concise hook;
- make each slide understandable quickly;
- avoid dense academic wording;
- build one coherent argument rather than unrelated tips;
- preserve important evidence nuance;
- use the caption for useful context rather than repeating every slide;
- avoid hashtag spam.

slide_number must be sequential starting at 1.

Use an empty string when headline, emphasis, or CTA is intentionally
omitted.
